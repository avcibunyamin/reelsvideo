ReelsVideo Studio

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

ReelsVideo Studio is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner pick their own videos, write a title and description once, and publish them to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok through each platform's official API.

How it works
  • The app runs locally (on 127.0.0.1); there is no ReelsVideo server and no account to create.
  • You connect your own platform accounts with their official sign-in (OAuth). Tokens are stored only on your computer.
  • Videos are uploaded only when you press publish, only to the accounts you connected.
