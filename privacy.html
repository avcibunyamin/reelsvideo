Last updated: 30 October 2026

ReelsVideo Studio ("the app") is a personal tool that runs entirely on the user's own computer. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

YouTube API Services. ReelsVideo Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube: with your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload only to upload the videos you choose, with the title and description you enter, to your own YouTube channel.
  • Instagram and TikTok: if you connect them, the app uses their official publishing APIs in the same way, only for the posts you create.
  • Your files: the videos you select and the texts you write stay on your computer.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and settings are stored only in a local file on your computer.
  • There is no ReelsVideo server. The app does not collect analytics and does not send your data to the developer or to any third party other than the platform you publish to.
  • The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads to YouTube are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete the stored YouTube keys in the app under Platform Ayarları, or delete the app's local data folder; this removes every token and setting at once. The app keeps no YouTube data other than these tokens and the results of your own uploads (video ID and link), which are deleted together with the post.
  • ReelsVideo Studio does not download, cache or store YouTube audiovisual content.
  • For questions or a deletion request, contact bunyaminiletisim@gmail.com; requests are answered within 7 days.
